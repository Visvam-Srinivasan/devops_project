# Incident Response Runbook

When an alert fires, the first responder should acknowledge it within five
minutes and open an incident channel. Capture a timeline of what was
observed, what actions were taken, and when the system returned to a
healthy state.

After the incident is resolved, schedule a blameless postmortem within two
business days while details are still fresh. The goal is to find gaps in
tooling or process, not to assign fault to an individual.
